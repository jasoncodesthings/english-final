About Choir


I have been in Stuyvesant's Bass Choir for all three years, and boy, joining this was the best decision I have ever made in my life.


Being in choir is one of the most fun things you can experience in Stuy. Yes, there are lots of after-school rehersals. Yes, those rehersals and concerts are quite intense. However, you will make the most wonderful friends, have countless funny moments, and overall, an awesome time there.


If I could rewind time, I would do everything all over again. Having dinner at midnight post-concert. Gaining aura from wearing a concert black suit to class. Messing around with friends at the piano. Everything. I'd rewind time to 8th grade so I could get into Stuy a year earlier and experience an additional year of it. Choir gave me an identity to who I am and made my overall experience at Stuy much more sophisticated.
